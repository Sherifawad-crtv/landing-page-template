01

Overview

This document explains how SEE Padel (Sports Education Enhancer) ("SEE Padel", "we", "us", or "our") manages personal data and governs your use of our applications, dashboards, and websites (collectively, the "Services").

SEE Padel complies with:

  • Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 (PDPL)
  • UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (UAE PDPL)

and is prepared for alignment with:

  • EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR 2016/679) — future application once SEE Padel serves EU residents
  • African data-protection laws such as Kenya's DPA 2019, Nigeria's NDPR 2019, and South Africa's POPIA 2013

SEE Padel operates from Egypt and currently has no EU representative.

02

What we collect

Account data

Name, email, phone, password (hashed), user role.

Athlete performance & educational data

Age, gender, sport, test results, session history, attendance, and optional health or injury data (processed only with explicit consent).

Device & usage data

IP, device ID, OS version, analytics metrics, crash logs.

Payment data

Subscription records, handled by PCI-compliant processors. SEE Padel never stores full card numbers.

Communication data

Chat, feedback, survey, and support messages.

03

How we use data

  • Deliver, maintain, and improve the Services.
  • Operate analytics and educational insights.
  • Provide customer support and safety monitoring.
  • Conduct lawful marketing (opt-in only).
  • Fulfil contracts and legal obligations.

Legal bases for processing

  • Consent — especially for sensitive or optional data.
  • Contractual necessity — providing the Services.
  • Legitimate interest — quality control, fraud prevention.
  • Legal obligation — compliance with PDPL, UAE PDPL, and tax laws.
04

Children and minors

Parental or guardian consent is required for users under 18. Consent is obtained through a verified parent/guardian checkbox and email confirmation workflow. Guardians may access or delete their child's data via written request to support@see.com.co.

06

Sharing & transfers

Data is shared only with authorized clubs and schools, vendors, and analytics partners bound by contracts. Sub-processors include secure cloud platforms (e.g. AWS, GCP, Azure). A list of current service providers is available upon request or through the app.

Data may be stored in or transferred to Egypt, the UAE, or other jurisdictions with adequate safeguards. SEE Padel uses encryption and contractual protections, and will adopt Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) if transferring to the EEA in future.

07

Your rights

You may request to access, correct, erase, or restrict processing of your data, withdraw consent, or receive a copy of your data. Submit requests via the in-app portal or email support@see.com.co. SEE Padel responds within 30 days unless an extension is legally allowed.

08

Security & breaches

  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Role-based access control
  • Continuous monitoring and incident response
  • Staff training and auditing

SEE Padel notifies the Egyptian and/or UAE regulators within 72 hours of any breach likely to cause risk, and notifies affected users without undue delay.

09

Marketing, cookies & future jurisdictions

Marketing emails are sent only with opt-in consent. Cookies and SDKs collect functional and analytics data; you may disable them through your browser or device settings. SEE Padel may apply GDPR and African data-protection principles as applicable.
